## Page 2 – Full Speech Script

### Title: *AI as Your Everyday Health and Trust Partner*

**Opening (0:30–0:50)**  
“Good morning, fellow Toastmasters and guests.  

Raise your hand if you’ve ever seen a health ad that told you to ‘exercise more’ or ‘eat better’—and then forgot about you the next day.  

*(Pause, gesture to the audience.)*  

Today, I’m not here to talk about more billboards or generic slogans.  

I’m here to present our **AI‑powered Public Relations strategy** for building trust in three areas: **Health, Farming, and Daily Life.**  

Instead of shouting at the crowd, our strategy is to **listen, learn, and gently nudge** people toward healthier choices—using Artificial Intelligence as our partner.”  

---

**Health – AI as Your Personal Wellness Coach (1:15–1:30)**  
“Let’s start with **Health and Wellness.**  

In traditional PR, we send the same message to everyone: ‘Walk more, eat better.’  

With AI, that changes.  

Imagine a system that already knows you’re busy with kids and work, that you usually skip lunch, and that your sleep is light.  

Instead of a generic ‘exercise more,’ AI might suggest:  
- ‘Today, walk for 10 minutes at 3 PM while waiting for your child’s school bus.’  
- ‘Tomorrow, add a green vegetable to one meal; I’ll send you a simple recipe.’  

This isn’t just advice—it’s a **relationship.**  

AI learns your lifestyle, predicts where you might slip, and nudges you back on track.  

From a PR perspective, this builds **trust** because the message feels personal, not pushy.  

When people feel understood, they listen—and they start to trust the source.”  

---

**Farming – Smarter Soil, Healthier Harvests (1:30–1:45)**  
“Now, let’s move to **Farming**—where the soil is our patient.  

In the past, many farmers relied on guesswork or one‑size‑fits‑all fertilizer schedules.  

Our PR strategy uses AI to turn **soil analysis into a relationship** between us and every farmer. [web:27][web:30][web:33][web:36]  

AI‑powered tools can test soil in real time, measuring nutrients, pH, moisture, and organic matter.  

Then the AI suggests:  
- ‘Your soil is low on nitrogen; add this compost mix.’  
- ‘Rotate crops to this pattern for better organic yield.’  
- ‘Avoid this chemical—your soil type doesn’t need it.’  

This isn’t just data—it’s **trust.**  

When a farmer follows an AI‑backed recommendation and sees better yields without chemicals, they don’t just thank the machine.  

They thank the organization that gave them that tool.  

In PR terms, we’re not selling fertilizer; we’re **positioning ourselves as a partner in sustainable farming.**”  

---

**Daily Life – Small Habits, Big Impact (1:30–1:45)**  
“Finally, let’s talk about **our daily lives**—where most of our health decisions happen.  

AI doesn’t just track your steps; it can help you choose what to buy, what to eat, and how to plan your meals. [web:24][web:28][web:31][web:34]  

Imagine:  
- You’re shopping for a lotion. AI compares ingredients and suggests one that matches your skin type and aligns with your preference for fewer chemicals.  
- You’re in the grocery store. AI shows you a short list of healthy options that fit your budget and cooking time.  
- At home, AI helps you plan a weekly meal plan so you’re not tempted by last‑minute takeout.  

From a PR perspective, this is **reputation building.**  

When AI helps people stay on track with their health goals—without feeling judged or scolded—it creates a **positive, long‑term relationship** between the consumer and the brand.  

The message is simple:  
‘We’re not just here to sell you something.  
We’re here to help you live better.’”  

---

**Conclusion – The Future of PR is Personal (0:45–1:00)**  
“So, what’s the story we’re telling?  

We’re telling a story where **AI is not a cold machine, but a careful listener.**  

- In health, it listens to your habits and suggests small, realistic changes.  
- In farming, it listens to the soil and helps farmers grow more with less chemical input.  
- In daily life, it listens to your choices and nudges you toward healthier versions of yourself.  

As a PR strategist, our job isn’t to sell AI.  

Our job is to **use AI to deepen trust**—one personalized nudge at a time.  

I therefore propose this AI‑powered Public Relations strategy for the coming year.  

Let’s move from generic noise to **meaningful, personalized conversations**—with our patients, our farmers, and our everyday customers.  

Thank you.”

## Page 6 – Practice Tips & Timing Guide

### 1. Target Timing (5–7 minutes)

- **Opening:** 0:30–0:50 seconds  
- **Health segment:** 1:15–1:30 minutes  
- **Farming segment:** 1:30–1:45 minutes  
- **Daily Life segment:** 1:30–1:45 minutes  
- **Conclusion:** 0:45–1:00 minute  

### 2. Delivery Tips

- Use the hook: “Raise your hand if…” to connect early.  
- Pause after each example so the audience can picture it.  
- Smile when you say “Thank you”; end with clear, quiet confidence.  

### 3. Slide‑Use Reminders

- Use 1 slide per topic (5 slides total).  
- Keep bullets short; don’t read slides verbatim.  
- Show the slide once, then turn back to the audience.  

### 4. Pathway Alignment (Public Relations Strategies, Level 4)

- Frame this as a **public‑relations strategy**, not just a tech talk.  
- Emphasize how AI builds trust in **health, farming, and daily life**.  
- Highlight that you are “proposing a plan” to the audience (as required by the project).
