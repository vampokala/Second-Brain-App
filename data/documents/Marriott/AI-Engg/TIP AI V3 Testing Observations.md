

**response messages**

```
"response": "Are you asking about an early check-in at 2pm or a late checkout at 2pm? \n\nFor early check-in at 2pm:\n- We can check availability for you, but early check-in is subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed until the day of your arrival. Could you please provide your estimated time of arrival?\n\nFor late checkout at 2pm:\n- Late checkout is also subject to availability and is typically confirmed on the day of departure. Could you let us know your desired checkout time?\n\nIf you have elite status, it may influence the priority of your request.
```
